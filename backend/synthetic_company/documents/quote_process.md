# Northstar Quote Process

This fictional procedure describes the standard workflow for preparing a Northstar customer quote.

## Customer and requirement check

Sales looks up the customer in the CRM and confirms the billing entity, account owner, delivery location, and requested products. Missing customer details are resolved before a quote is prepared.

## Pricing and quote preparation

Sales retrieves the current standard product price from the pricing catalogue, records quantities and commercial terms, and creates a draft quote. The pricing catalogue remains the authoritative source for structured prices.

## Approval and issue

Any required discount approval must be obtained and recorded before the final quote is issued. Once approved, Sales sends the controlled final version to the customer.

## CRM follow-up

Sales records the issued quote, its date, total value, and approval reference in the CRM. The opportunity stage is updated, and the owner records the customer's response or next follow-up date.
