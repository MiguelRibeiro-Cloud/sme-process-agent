# Commercial Discount Policy

## Standard discounts

Standard customer quotes may be issued without management approval when the total discount is 10% or less.

## Sales Manager approval

Discounts above 10% require approval from the Sales Manager before the quote is sent to the customer.

## Commercial Director approval

Discounts above 20% require approval from both the Sales Manager and Commercial Director.

## Recording approval

Approval must be recorded before the final quote is issued.
