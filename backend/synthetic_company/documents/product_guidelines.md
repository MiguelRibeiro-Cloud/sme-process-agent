# Product Operations Guidelines

These fictional guidelines support product selection and quote preparation without replacing the structured pricing catalogue.

## Product fit

Sales should confirm the customer's operating environment, required capacity, and integration constraints before recommending a product. Uncertain technical fit should be referred to a Solutions Engineer.

## Availability and lead times

Availability and lead times must be checked before promising a delivery date. A standard price does not imply that stock is immediately available.

## Bundles and substitutions

Optional services and accessories should be listed separately on the quote. Product substitutions require confirmation that the alternative meets the documented customer requirement; price alone is not sufficient justification.

## Custom requirements

Requests for non-standard configuration, installation, or support terms must be reviewed by the relevant delivery owner before the quote is issued.
